v1.2.0 - Product Variations and Customer Email Normalization

## What's New

* **Storefront variations** — select product options such as colour and size, with availability handling for missing combinations and a variant dropdown for products without options.
* **Panel option management** — manage product options and their values through the Filament panel; view and edit generated product variants.
* **Demo variations** — seeded option combinations, colour hex metadata, price differences and out-of-stock examples, with idempotency coverage.

## Fixes and Maintenance

* **Customer email normalization** — trim whitespace and lowercase email addresses in customer authentication flows and the user model.
* **Existing customer emails** — migration normalizes stored addresses and stops on collisions rather than merging accounts.
* **Demo customer credentials** — reseeding preserves existing customer passwords and profiles.
* **CI configuration** — provide the required demo admin password only to the test step; customer password fixtures remain controlled by the tests.
* **Test coverage** — add seven Feature test files covering variations, panel options, email normalization and demo credentials.

## Upgrade Notes

* Back up the database before upgrading, then run `php artisan migrate --force` in the deployment environment.
* If email normalization reports a collision, resolve the affected accounts manually before retrying. The migration does not merge accounts; rollback does not restore original email casing or whitespace.
* Running DemoDataSeeder requires `EXPORTDEPO_ADMIN_PASSWORD` in the environment. Use an appropriate private value for your environment, not the CI-only test value. Seeding is not required merely to apply the migration.
